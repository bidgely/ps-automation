SQS Queues

Queues are sorted by messages available, highest first. Processed/min and incoming/min come from CloudWatch for the latest complete minute, which usually runs a few minutes behind. Trend compares the two: draining queues show an estimated time to empty at the current rate. Under All they form one combined list; under an environment they're grouped by service. Boxes is the number of instances running for the queue (hover for the source; "—" means none matched yet). The load bar fills up as a queue approaches and passes its red threshold. Click a queue to copy its URL. In the adhoc view, a queue is Free when it has nothing available, nothing in flight and nothing arriving; free queues are listed first.
